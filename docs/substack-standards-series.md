# Substack standards and series

## Cap standards

Cap standards for titles and headings: cap first word and proper names only.

## Series titles and tags

Substack is the *notification hub and narrative center* of the overall stack. Notifications for all new repo overviews and guides, and accompanying YouTube videos, are published here.

Initially (June 2026) there are four Substack series for the VRSG substack. As new series are developed they should be documented here. Each series gets a title and a tag:

- The series title is the lead text for each post title. The pattern is `[Series name]: [extended title]`, for example, *Welcome to the bench: Pflueger Nobby model 1963 baitcasting reel*. These are more creative and literary than the tags.

- The tag is assigned to the post "behind the scenes" and serves to group posts so that site visitors can easily see logical groupings using clear terms. For example, Welcome to the bench posts are tagged *new arrival*.

The four initial series titles, tags, and functions:

  - **Welcome to the bench:** When a new reel comes to the bench, a bit about why and how it was acquired, plans are for it. Tag = New Arrival

  - **Production notes:** Occasional feature. How images, videos, and other content are made, designed, and managed, on the bench and on the computer. Tag = Production Notes

  - **Reel stories:** When service is completed on a reel, a story about taking it into the field, how it performs, where it is integrated with other tackle to make a balanced outfit, what fish are caught. Also stories about how a reel is significant, esp. in my life, for example, the hero post for substack, about my first reel. Tag = Field Stories

  - **Service guide:** Basically a simple notification. When a new overview + guide + video stack is completely published, a notification. Tag = Guides

Refer to skeletons, etc. by series name, tag is **only** used in ss.

## Workflow for Welcome posts

  - Use skeleton in Substack.

  - Post to substack, tag as "new arrival."

  - Wire to repo: Post template includes a Connections section with link to the repo, notes that when the entire stack is published a notification will be posted on substack.

  - Wire to YT: Connections section includes link to YT. Optionally create substack video for posting to YouTube shorts.
