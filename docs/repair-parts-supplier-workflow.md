# Repair / parts supplier reference workflow

This page explains how parts, suppliers, and repairs are investigated, tested, documented, and promoted to the public site.

For any specific repair, a **repair-notes page** will be created in the project for the specific reel and added to the nav for that reel. Any future repairs for that reel will be added to the same page. For example: penn-720-repair-notes.md

A short inspection note and link to the repair notes page will be added to the **service guide**.

## Supplier listing verification

For any new parts supplier, notes will be added to the workspace supplier-reference.md to track them until parts are obtained, used in a repair, tested, and passed.

After passing, but before adding a supplier or seller to the public Vintage Reel Parts page:

1. Confirm that the part has met the project’s obtain, install, bench-test, and field-test publication standard.

2. Contact the supplier when practical and ask whether they are comfortable being listed as a resource on Vintage Reel Service Guides.

3. Confirm the preferred public link, such as a website, eBay store, seller profile, or a specific listing.

4. Record the date of confirmation and any requested wording, attribution, or limitations in the workspace supplier-reference.md

5. Make clear in VRSG that the listing is informational and does not imply endorsement (this standard wording may be added to the repo skeleton). e.g.:

    Published observations apply to the tested reel and configuration only. They do not guarantee compatibility, performance, or durability in every reel of the same model or family.

6. Recheck the link and listing status periodically, especially before a major guide revision.

After this verification process:

  - The supplier's information will be added to the vetted suppliers in the workspace supplier-reference.md

  - An annotated reference will be added to the parts page. This page is not necessarily or primarily linked from any other page, it is included in the nav under references and serves a similar function.

## Parts / repair workflow

1. Photograph the replacement beside the cracked original, including both faces and any molded, machined, or printed markings. Save these images in the media folder for the appropriate reel.

2. Record the supplier’s stated model application, material description, purchase date, and item or listing identifier in the workspace supplier-reference.md

3. Compare major functional features before installation: thickness, slot or track geometry, pin-hole diameter, bearing surfaces, and overall finish.

4. Photograph the installed component before closing the housing.

5. Run a dry, low-load mechanical check before final lubrication and assembly.

6. Bench-test winding, spool reciprocation, anti-reverse operation, bail/rotor operation, and any interference through multiple cycles.

7. Complete your defined first field test.

8. Update the private repair capsule first; then promote the tested, dated observation to the repair note and annotated parts page.
