## Penn 722

**Purchased**: 9.8.26, $50.00, ebay, seller: thereeldoc (seller not recommended, see below).

**Description**: spinning reel, blue / teal / white, one of Penn's Spinfisher series.

**Condition**: very good, some obvious wear to the finish in seller images.

### Evaluation

*after receiving*

Overall: Fair to good.

Frame / case: Solid, no serious corrosion, dents, or other damage.

Handle / knobs: Good, white plastic handle good, crank good.

Spool / drag cap: Fair to good, drag cap has some cracks, not unusual for these reels.

Badges / logos / decals: Fair to good, a bit tarnished but should clean up well.

Finish / paint: Fair, a good bit of wear to the paint finish. Looks like normal usage.

Inside case: Poor to fair, packed very full of what appears to be old grease, brown. Needs service.

Initial bench tests: Cranks smoothly in both directions, anti-rev works, clicker does not (probably clogged with grease, may work after cleaning), drag cap seems to work, spool comes off easily, bail flips.

### Service / parts / repairs

9.12.26: This reel will be opened, inspected, and tested but it appears to have been serviced already.

9.15.26: This seller stated this reel was "quick serviced" and "everything works" which are not quite accurate. The clicker does not work and the inside has either not been serviced recently, or has been serviced wrong. Far too much grease, and appears to not be reel grease.

### Field use

Rated for 2 - 10 lb test line, planning to load it with 8 lb.

This reel spans the light, medium-light, and medium classes. This range makes it suitable for target species from panfish to large bass.

### Documentation

9.12.26: This reel should be documented as a variation on the 720, so adding a note in the 720 sg and a pointer / link to a separate doc, e.g., penn-722-notes.md, where the bearing vs. bushing difference is documented.

This would be a good place to comparing the bushing vs. bearing versions of the same reel. The 722 / bearing version is supposed to be smoother retrieve.

### Notes

This reel was purchased as a sister to the Penn 720 - this is the bearing version vs. the 720 with bushings, otherwise mechanically identical - so parts can be traded between them, spools also. Initially motivated as part of a search to replace the 720's cracked crosswind block.

## Penn 710

**Purchased**: 9.10.26, $50.00, ebay, seller: uppercreek.

**Description**: spinning reel, green / white, one of Penn's Spinfisher series.

**Condition**: very good, some obvious wear to the finish in seller images.

### Evaluaion

*after receiving*

Overall: Good to very good.

Frame / case: Solid, no serious corrosion, dents, or other damage.

Handle / knobs: Good, white plastic handle good, crank good.

Spool / drag cap: Fair to good, drag cap is good, may be a non-OEM replacement (seems a little too perfect).

Badges / logos / decals: Fair to good, a bit tarnished but should clean up well.

Finish / paint: Fair to good, a good bit of wear to the paint finish. Looks like normal usage.

Inside case: Good, looks like it has been serviced relatively recently and correctly, some grease, green, looks like reel grease.

Initial bench tests: Cranks smoothly in both directions, anti-rev and clicker work, drag cap seems to work, spool comes off easily, bail flips.

### Service / parts / repairs

9.12.26: Looking ahead, reel is arriving in the next few days. This reel will be opened, inspected, and tested but it appears to have been serviced already.

### Field use

This reel is rated for line weights from 8 - 20 lbs, although for most rods it is rated up to 15 lbs, so for an older reel it might be wise to consider 15 lbs a more appropriate upper limit than 20 - this still places it in the saltwater / surf / boat class. Plan to load it with 14 or 15 lb line.

This reel spans the medium-light, medium, medium-heavy / saltwater class, and heavy duty ratings. This wide range makes it suitable for target species from smaller bass to tarpon, surf and boat fishing.

### Documentation

9.12.26: This reel should be documented fully for Volume 2 of the VRSG series as the next size up from the 720 / 722.

### Notes

This is the bushing version of the Penn Spinfisher 710 / 711 set. Otherwise mechanically identical. An iconic "greenie."
