# Vintage rod log

## St. Croix Pacemaker 400 HD

**Purchased**: 8.19.26, $9.98, ebay, tf*finds.

**Description**: 4'6" one-piece, baitcasting rod, fiberglass, teal with dark blue guide wraps.

**Condition**: Seller photographs indicate an unusually clean, apparently complete and substantially original example. Final condition assessment pending hands-on inspection.

### Evaluation

**Overall**: Very good, perfectly functional with minor blemishes.

**Blank**: Very good, a few minor scratches, teal.

**Guides**: Light rust. Localized rust and plating loss on frame, particularly near the lower shoulder / foot area. Most of the rust is on the inside of the frames.

Tip-top is almost rust-free, shows remnants of a clear amber coating, mostly gone. Bit of this appear on the other guides too.

**Reel seat**: Light wear with some bare metal showing through paint (no rust), dark / cobalt blue.

**Guide wraps**: Very good, original dark blue wraps with light accent color.

**Decals**: Fair with some worn spots.

**Collet**: Unscrews OK. Blank to reel seat connection looks good, minimal corrosion.

**Bushings**: 4 metal strips.

**Reel lock**: Works, utilizes a rubber knob and screw head. Some minor rust.

**Butt cap**: Original, very dark blue hard rubber.

### Restoration / conservation

The whole rod was given a scrub with a soft brush and very diluted Dawn detergent.

The hard rubber was cleaned with #0000 steel wool and fiber pads and given a light coating of '303' rubber preservative.

The guides were lightly cleaned with #0000 steel wool and a wooden pick. Some of the rust is very difficult to get to, so was left alone rather than risk damage to the guide wraps.

## Reel-seat and locking mechanism

The trigger-style reel seats for casting rods have a number of variable components for locking the reel in place. *Threaded locking-hood assembly* is a good general term, but for precision in documenting these, this table provides a breakdown of most possibilities.

| Part or assembly | Preferred term | What it means |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Entire component that holds the reel | Reel seat | The complete assembly, including the body/barrel, trigger, fixed hood, movable hood, threaded area, and nut |
| Stationary metal cap over one reel foot | Fixed hood | The non-moving hood, generally at one end of the seat |
| Moving metal cap that clamps the other reel foot | Locking hood or movable hood | The hood that advances over the reel foot when tightened |
| Threaded cylindrical piece turned by hand | Locking nut | The part that travels on the threaded barrel and advances or retracts the locking hood  |
| Threaded portion under the nut | Threaded barrel or reel-seat threads | The male threads that permit adjustment |
| Finger projection under the seat | Trigger | The integral casting-control projection |
| Full system in a condition note | Threaded locking-hood assembly | Useful when you want to specify the mechanism without over-describing every part |
